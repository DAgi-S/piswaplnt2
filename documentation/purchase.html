Overview

The Purchase Management System allows users to manage purchase transactions, including creating new purchases, editing existing ones, and generating reports. The system integrates with the supplier and product management systems.

Managing Purchases

The main purchase management interface displays a table of all purchases with the following information:

  • Purchase Number
  • Purchase Date
  • Supplier Information
  • Sub Total
  • VAT (15%)
  • Grand Total
  • Status
  • Action buttons (Edit, Remove)

Adding a Purchase

To add a new purchase:

  1. Click the "Add Purchase" button
  2. Select a supplier from the dropdown
  3. Enter the purchase date
  4. Add products using the "Add Row" button
  5. For each product:
    • Select the product from the dropdown
    • Enter the quantity
    • The rate will be automatically filled
    • The total will be calculated automatically
  6. The sub total, VAT, and grand total are calculated automatically
  7. Click "Save Changes" to create the purchase

Editing a Purchase

To edit an existing purchase:

  1. Click the "Action" button for the purchase you want to edit
  2. Select "Edit" from the dropdown
  3. Modify the purchase details as needed
  4. Add or remove product rows as necessary
  5. Click "Save Changes" to update the purchase

Removing a Purchase

To remove a purchase:

  1. Click the "Action" button for the purchase you want to remove
  2. Select "Remove" from the dropdown
  3. Confirm the deletion in the popup dialog
Note: Removed purchases are not permanently deleted but marked as inactive in the database.

Purchase Report

The Purchase Report feature allows users to generate detailed reports of purchase transactions. To generate a report:

  1. Navigate to "Purchase Report" in the menu
  2. Select the date range for the report:
    • Start Date
    • End Date
  3. Optionally select a specific supplier from the dropdown
  4. Click "Generate Report" to view the report

Report Contents

The generated report includes:

  • Report header with period and supplier information
  • Detailed purchase information:
    • Purchase date
    • Purchase number
    • Supplier details
    • Items purchased with quantities
    • Sub total
    • VAT amount
    • Total amount
  • Summary total at the bottom
  • Report generation details (date/time and user)

Email Purchase Report

Users can email purchase reports directly from the system. To send a report via email:

  1. Generate a report as described above
  2. Enter the recipient's email address in the "Email To" field
  3. Click "Send Email Report"

Email Features

  • HTML formatted email with proper styling
  • Includes all report details in a clean, tabular format
  • Alternative text version for email clients that don't support HTML
  • Automatic email validation
  • Success/error notifications

Important Notes:

  • Email configuration uses SMTP with SSL/TLS encryption
  • Reports are sent from swapcapital@lebawi.net
  • Large reports may take a few moments to generate and send
  • Check spam folder if the email is not received in the inbox
