Threshold Settings Documentation

Overview

Threshold Settings allow you to configure how the system monitors and alerts about low stock levels. These settings help in maintaining optimal inventory levels and preventing stockouts.

Parameters

1. Threshold Buffer (%)

This setting determines the buffer percentage above the minimum stock level at which the system starts generating alerts.

  • Default value: 20%
  • Example: If minimum stock is 100 units and buffer is 20%, alerts will trigger at 120 units
  • Range: 0-100%

2. Auto-calculate Thresholds

When enabled, the system automatically calculates recommended threshold levels based on:

  • Historical usage patterns
  • Lead time for restocking
  • Seasonal variations

3. Email Notifications

Configure email alerts for low stock situations:

  • Receive notifications when items reach threshold levels
  • Customize recipients for different types of alerts
  • Option to disable email notifications

4. Alert Frequency

Choose how often you receive notifications:

  • Immediate: Get alerts as soon as thresholds are reached
  • Daily: Receive a daily summary of low stock items
  • Weekly: Get a weekly report of inventory status

Stock Level Categories

Out of Stock

Current stock = 0

Critical Stock

Current stock ≤ 50% of minimum stock

Low Stock

Current stock ≤ minimum stock

Best Practices

  • Regularly review and adjust threshold levels based on business needs
  • Consider seasonal variations when setting buffer percentages
  • Use auto-calculate feature for high-volume items
  • Set appropriate alert frequencies to avoid notification fatigue

Note:

Changes to threshold settings take effect immediately and will update the status indicators in the low stock table.
